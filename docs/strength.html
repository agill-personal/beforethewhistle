Strength Training

Stronger players are faster, more confident, and harder to knock off the ball, and this section is all about building that foundation.

With Coach Kat (Brookline coach and certified strength and conditioning specialist), players will learn how to train safely, effectively, and with purpose. Early in June, we will run an introductory session focused on learning proper form and understanding how strength training supports performance on the field.

From there, we will build a simple, effective program tailored to this group.

The below strength plan is a rough example of what will likely be recommended to the girls. However, details of the plan are still tentative until we host the initial strength training intro session.

Rest Between Sets60–90 sec for strength sets; 2 min for heavier lifts in Phase 3
Warm-UpAlways 5–8 min: leg swings, hip circles, glute bridges, and a light jog
ProgressionAdd weight or reps each week only if last session felt controlled throughout
Form FirstNever sacrifice form for load. A clean bodyweight squat beats a sloppy weighted one.
