Technical Sessions

Technical Sessions

These are your chances to get real touches, real reps, and real improvement. Each week features five different technical themes designed to help players sharpen specific parts of their game and make noticeable progress over the summer.

Come as often as your schedule allows. Some players will show up once in a while, others will make it part of their weekly rhythm, and both are completely fine. The only expectation is that when you do show up, you are ready to work, compete, and get better.

Bring a ball, cleats, and a good attitude. That's it.

Themes
First Touch & Receiving

Controlling the ball under pressure, using the first touch to set up the next action, and receiving cleanly in tight spaces and difficult body positions.

Passing & Combination Play

Short and long passing, weight and timing of the pass, playing through pressure, and linking with teammates through combinations and movement off the ball.

1v1 Attacking vs. Defending

Attacking with pace, feints, and changes of direction to beat a defender, and defending with patience, body shape, and timing to win the ball back.

Finishing vs. Defensive Pressure

Shooting technique from various angles and distances, composure and decision-making in the box, and converting chances with a defender closing in.

Crossing & Aerial Duels

Delivering accurate balls from wide positions, timing runs to meet the cross, and winning aerial duels at the near and far post.
